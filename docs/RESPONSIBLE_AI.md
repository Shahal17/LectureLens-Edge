# Responsible AI Notes

## Product rules

1. Never present generated notes as a perfect record of the lecture.
2. Link learning content to transcript evidence wherever possible.
3. Decline unsupported questions rather than fill gaps with plausible text.
4. Store nothing by default after the local session ends.
5. Clearly distinguish the portable reference engine from an NPU model.
6. Ask the student to verify high-stakes educational content against the lecturer
   or official material.

## Known limitations

- The reference engine is extractive and cannot reason like a language model.
- A glossary entry is learning support, not a complete translation.
- Complexity signals are cues for review, not a diagnosis of comprehension.
- Speech recognition quality will vary with accent, noise, microphone, and topic.
- Hardware-accelerated performance has not been measured in this development
  environment.

## Suggested pilot consent statement

“This prototype processes the lecture locally. Please obtain the lecturer's
permission before recording. Do not record private conversations. You may delete
the session at any time by closing the app without exporting.”
