# 90-Second Demo Checklist

## Before recording

- Run `py run.py`.
- Use Chrome or Edge at 100% zoom.
- Close unrelated tabs and disable notifications.
- Confirm the status pill says **Local reference engine** or **Snapdragon NPU ready**.
- Do not claim NPU execution unless the runtime panel actually detects QNN.

## Recording sequence

1. **0–10 seconds:** Show the landing statement and say the use case.
2. **10–18 seconds:** Click **Load demo lecture**.
3. **18–27 seconds:** Click **Analyse privately**.
4. **27–43 seconds:** Point to the summary, key concepts, source IDs, and `0 cloud calls`.
5. **43–55 seconds:** Show Confusion Radar.
6. **55–68 seconds:** Ask `Why should we keep test data separate?` and show its source.
7. **68–78 seconds:** Open **Quiz** and answer one question.
8. **78–85 seconds:** Open **Accessibility**, show the Malayalam glossary and large-text mode.
9. **85–90 seconds:** Open **Evidence** and finish on the privacy receipt.

## One-sentence close

“LectureLens Edge gives every student a private second chance to understand the
lecture—even when the internet is unavailable.”
